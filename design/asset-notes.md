## Original generation brief

Create a premium photorealistic architectural website background inspired by the atmosphere and poster composition of The Dark Knight Rises. Look straight upward between immense angular black skyscrapers and fractured architectural slabs. Buildings converge toward a small cold silver-white opening high on the right. Use detailed concrete and metal, icy rim light, subtle fog and volumetric shafts, a few tiny ember sparks, and deep near-black, charcoal, graphite and desaturated steel blue. Keep the left half and lower middle dark for portfolio typography. No people, text, UI, or watermark.

## Interaction

Cursor illumination is 70% of the approved draft's strength. Panel highlights are also set to 70%. The hero has layered parallax and projected 3D fragments; it is not a fully modelled city. Motion stops at rest, respects the operating system's reduced-motion preference, and can be disabled with the footer control. The control's preference is stored locally when storage is available.
